ZERObot Premium

We're working on optional premium features for servers that want a little extra. Nothing is for sale yet, and ZERObot works great without it.

What we're exploring

These are ideas, not promises. Plans and features may change before launch.

Customization

Custom welcome messages, level-up cards, and other ways to make ZERObot feel like your server's own.

Higher limits

More room for things like auto-roles, polls, and saved history.

Advanced tools

Extra management and community features for larger or busier servers.

Free stays free

The core features you use today, like moderation, leveling, and fun commands, are meant to stay free. Premium would only add extras on top.
