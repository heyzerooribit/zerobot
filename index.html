Server management

Keep your server safe and tidy

Moderation tools that respect your role setup, so mods can act fast without stepping on anyone above them.

  • Kick, ban, and time out members with a reason.
  • Warn members and keep a warning history for your team.
  • Clear up to 100 messages at once with /purge.
  • Built-in checks stop mods from acting on higher roles.
/warn @member reasonSaved
/timeout @member 3030 min
/purge 25Done

Community

Make your members feel at home

Welcome people, reward activity, and get everyone talking.

  • Levels and XP with a server leaderboard.
  • Custom welcome messages and an automatic role for new members.
  • Quick reaction polls with up to four options.
1. Member oneLevel 12
2. Member twoLevel 9
3. Member threeLevel 7

Entertainment

A little fun between the serious stuff

Quick commands that give your channels something to do.

  • Flip a coin or roll any die.
  • Can't decide? Let ZERObot choose for you.

/8ball Will we hit 100 members?
