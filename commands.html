Commands

ZERObot uses slash commands. Type / in any channel of your server to see them, then pick one from the list.

Server management

Moderation tools. Discord hides these from members who don't have the permission. ZERObot also refuses to act on anyone whose top role is equal to or above yours or its own.

CommandWhat it doesWho can use it
/kick member [reason]Remove a member from the server. They can rejoin with an invite.Kick Members
/ban member [reason]Ban a member from the server.Ban Members
/timeout member minutes [reason]Temporarily mute a member for up to 28 days.Moderate Members
/warn member reasonWarn a member. ZERObot saves it and tries to DM them.Moderate Members
/warnings memberSee a member's warning history.Moderate Members
/clearwarnings memberRemove all of a member's warnings.Moderate Members
/purge amountDelete 1 to 100 recent messages in the current channel.Manage Messages

Community

Members earn XP for chatting (at most once per minute) and level up over time. ZERObot counts messages but never stores what they say.

CommandWhat it doesWho can use it
/rank [member]Show your level, XP, and server rank (or someone else's).Everyone
/leaderboardShow the top 10 members by XP.Everyone
/poll question option1 option2 [option3] [option4]Start a poll that members vote on with reactions.Everyone
/setwelcome channel [message]Send a welcome message in a channel when someone joins. Use {user} and {server} in the message.Manage Server
/welcome_offTurn off welcome messages.Manage Server
/setautorole roleGive new members a role automatically.Manage Roles
