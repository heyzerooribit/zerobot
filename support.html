Before you ask

Most problems are answered on the FAQ page: commands not showing up, permission errors, and role order.

Reporting a bug

The more detail, the faster we can fix it. Please include:

  • The command you used and what you expected to happen.
  • What actually happened, plus any error message the bot showed.
  • Your server ID and roughly when it happened (a screenshot helps).

Never send your bot token or any password.
